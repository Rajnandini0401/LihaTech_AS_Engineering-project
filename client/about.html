About A S Engineering Work

COMPANY PROFILE

Pioneers in Stainless Steel Precision Engineering

A S Engineering Work is a precision metal engineering, machining, and stainless steel fabrication company based in Jakhinwadi, Tal. Karad, Dist. Satara, Maharashtra.

Our company profile is built on over 15+ years of dedicated engineering excellence. We produce prime quality SS rings, SS bushings, submersible pump sleeves, seamless SS pipes, and custom drawing-based machined assemblies for industrial OEMs, hydraulic pump makers, and chemical processing facilities across India.
